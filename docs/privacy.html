Privacy Policy

GrapeCompare is designed to compare files privately on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

GrapeCompare does not collect personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. The app does not require an account.

Files and folders

Files and folders you select are read and compared locally on your device. Their contents and metadata are not uploaded to the developer or any third party.

Demo data

The optional demo files and folders are generated locally by the app and remain on your Mac. Loading demo data does not initiate a network request.

Third-party services

GrapeCompare does not include analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. It does not use third-party cloud processing.

Data retention and deletion

Because no data is collected by the developer, there is no server-side personal data to retain or delete. You can remove locally generated demo data by deleting the app's caches or uninstalling the app.

Children

GrapeCompare does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
