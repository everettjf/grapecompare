Privacy Policy

GrapeCompare is designed to compare files privately on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Data collection

GrapeCompare does not collect personal information, usage analytics, diagnostics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data. The app does not require an account.

Files and folders

App Sandbox limits GrapeCompare to files and folders you explicitly select. They are read and compared locally on your device. Their contents and metadata are not uploaded to the developer or any third party.

Data stored on your Mac

GrapeCompare stores app preferences, security-scoped bookmarks for comparisons you choose to keep available, recent comparison names, and transactional operation journals inside its sandbox container. This local data is used only to provide app features and is never transmitted off-device.

Demo data

The optional demo files and folders are generated locally by the app and remain on your Mac. Loading demo data does not initiate a network request.

Third-party services

GrapeCompare does not include analytics, advertising, social, or tracking SDKs. It does not use third-party cloud processing.

Data retention and deletion

Because no data is collected by the developer, there is no server-side personal data to retain or delete. You can clear recent comparisons and operation history in the app. Uninstalling GrapeCompare and removing its sandbox container deletes its remaining local preferences, bookmarks, journals, and demo data.

Children

GrapeCompare does not knowingly collect data from children or any other users.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released.
